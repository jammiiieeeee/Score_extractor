## Design Specificity Verdict

The ConfigTab is indistinguishable from any video-tool's settings panel. The "Master's Studio" vision — ebony lacquer, warm brass controls glowing in ambient dim — is entirely absent. The heading reads "Settings," the groups are generic ("Detection," "Capture Timing," "Deduplication," "OCR," "Advanced"). Spin boxes are off-the-shelf QDoubleSpinBox with no visual treatment suggesting a studio console's tuning knobs. A music teacher opening this tab would feel they've entered a spreadsheet, not a precision instrument for their craft.
