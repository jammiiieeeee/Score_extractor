Clean Architecture as a Restaurant

The Restaurant Analogy

Customer orders
→
Waiter takes order
→
Kitchen manager coordinates
→
Chef cooks
→
Dish served
API Layer
The Waiter / Host
"Takes orders from customers, brings food back, handles payments."
Waiter: PyQt6 GUI, CLI, Web endpoints
Order pad: User input validation
Serving food: Displaying results to user
Key Point: The waiter doesn't cook! They just communicate between customer and kitchen. They know how to take an order, not how to make the dish.
Application Layer
The Kitchen Manager / Head Chef
"Receives the order, knows the workflow, coordinates the stations, but doesn't actually cook."
Manager: Use cases (ExtractScoreUseCase)
Workflow: "First prep, then cook, then plate"
Delegation: Calls video_service.open_video(), not cv2.VideoCapture()
Key Point: The manager knows the recipe steps but doesn't touch the knives. They say "prep station, do your job" — they don't know if it's a gas or electric stove.
Infrastructure Layer
The Kitchen Equipment & Line Cooks
"The actual stoves, ovens, knives, and cooks who do the work."
Gas stove: OpenCV (video processing)
Knife set: PaddleOCR (text recognition)
Plating station: ReportLab (PDF generation)
Line cooks: Concrete service implementations
Key Point: If the gas stove breaks, you swap in an electric one — the manager doesn't care, as long as it cooks. The interface is "stove that heats," not "GasBrand XYZ-2000."
Domain Layer
The Restaurant's Business Rules
"What IS a dish? What IS a customer? What are the rules? No equipment, no staff — just pure concepts."
Menu items: Frame, ScoreConfig (models)
Recipe rules: Business logic (deduplication)
"Cooking station": IVideoService (interface)
"Must have protein": Domain constraints
Key Point: The menu says "Grilled Salmon" — it doesn't say which brand of grill or which chef cooks it. It's the concept of the dish, not the implementation.

The Golden Rule

The Domain layer (business rules) never imports from Infrastructure (equipment).

Just like a menu doesn't say "Grilled on a Weber Genesis II" — it just says "Grilled Salmon."

If your Domain layer imports cv2 or paddleocr, you've broken the rule!
